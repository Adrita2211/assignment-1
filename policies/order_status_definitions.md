# Order Status Definitions

"processing" means the order is being prepared and has not shipped; typical
processing time is 1-2 business days. "in_transit" means the carrier has the
package and it is moving toward the delivery address. "delayed" means the
carrier has missed the original estimated delivery date. "delivered" means the
carrier confirmed drop-off. "delivered_damaged" and "lost_in_transit" are
exception statuses handled under the lost-or-damaged-package policy, not the
standard delivery timeline.
