# Shipping Delay Compensation

If an order's status shows "delayed" and it has not shipped within 5 business days
of its original estimated delivery date, the customer is entitled to a $10 shipping
credit applied to their account, automatically stackable up to $30 per rolling
90-day period. If the delay exceeds 15 business days past the estimated delivery
date, the customer may instead choose a full refund without needing to return
anything, since the item is presumed lost. Weather-related delays follow the
same compensation schedule once the delay is confirmed by the carrier. This
policy covers domestic shipping only.
