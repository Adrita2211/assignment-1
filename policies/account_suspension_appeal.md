# Account Suspension Appeals

Accounts move to "flagged" status pending review (e.g. suspected chargeback abuse
or a payment dispute) and to "suspended" status after 3 consecutive failed payment
attempts or a confirmed policy violation. A flagged or suspended customer may file
a written appeal, which is reviewed within 5 business days. During review, flagged
accounts can still place orders; suspended accounts cannot place new orders but
can still view order history and request refunds on past orders. Appeals are
submitted by escalating the ticket to the account-review queue; support agents
cannot lift a suspension directly.
