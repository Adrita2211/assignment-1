# Lost or Damaged Packages

Orders marked "lost_in_transit" by the carrier are automatically eligible for a full
refund or a free reshipment, customer's choice, with no return required. Orders
marked "delivered_damaged" require the customer to submit the issue within 14
days of the delivery date; a photo is recommended but not required to process the
refund or replacement. Support agents should offer a replacement first for
in-stock items, and a refund if the item is out of stock or the customer prefers
a refund.
