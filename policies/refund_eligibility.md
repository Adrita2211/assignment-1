# Refund Eligibility

Customers are eligible for a full refund if the request is made within 30 days of the
delivery date and the item is unused or in its original condition. Items marked
"delivered_damaged" or "lost_in_transit" are eligible for a full refund regardless of
the 30-day window, as long as the customer reports the issue within 14 days of the
delivery date (or expected delivery date, for lost packages). Refunds are issued to
the original payment method and typically post within 5-7 business days after
approval. Digital goods and gift cards are non-refundable.

Refund requests for orders totaling $150 or more require review and approval by a
human agent before the refund is issued, regardless of eligibility category. Refunds
under $150 that meet the eligibility criteria above may be approved without
additional review. A refund can only be issued for an order that has actually been
delivered (or is "delivered_damaged" / "lost_in_transit") -- an order still "in_transit"
or "processing" is not yet eligible for any refund, no matter its amount.
